Faces of Parkrun

A photography project documenting participants of Parkrun events in Krakow, Poland, and surrounding towns. The series captures both individuals and groups in mid-run, showcasing the joy of running alongside moments of personal effort. In this project, I do not aim for visual consistency; rather, I explore various post-processing styles and moods, tailoring the aesthetic of the images to the weather, the location, and the specific vibe of each run.
